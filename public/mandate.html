Terms of engagement

1.Appointment and authority

The Client appoints the Adviser to act as its broker and financial adviser to source, negotiate and arrange debt finance for the Transaction described above, and authorises the Adviser to act on the Client's behalf in doing so. This mandate confirms the Client's instructions and the basis on which the Adviser is engaged.

2.Scope of engagement

The Adviser will assist the Client to obtain finance for the Transaction, including preparing and submitting a loan application to the Lender, liaising with the Lender on the Client's behalf, and assisting the Client through to settlement of the finance. The Adviser arranges finance only; the Adviser does not lend money, and does not guarantee that finance will be approved or offered on any particular terms.

3.Authority to act and exchange information

The Client authorises the Adviser to: (a) act as the Client's agent in dealings with the Lender and any other party relevant to the Transaction; (b) collect, hold, use and disclose the Client's personal and financial information for the purpose of arranging the finance; and (c) request and receive information about the Client and the Transaction from the Lender, and from the Client's solicitor, accountant and other relevant parties.

4.Adviser fee

In consideration for the Adviser's services, the Client agrees to pay the Adviser the fee set out in section 1 above (the “Adviser Fee”). The Adviser Fee is earned on, and payable at, settlement or drawdown of the finance for the Transaction.

The Client authorises and directs that the Adviser Fee be paid to the Adviser from the loan proceeds through the Lender on settlement, and authorises the Adviser to invoice the Lender for the Adviser Fee accordingly. If for any reason the Adviser Fee is not paid through the Lender on settlement, the Client remains liable to pay the Adviser Fee to the Adviser directly within 7 days of settlement.

5.Other remuneration and disclosure

In addition to the Adviser Fee, the Adviser may receive commission from the Lender in connection with the finance. The Adviser will disclose the nature and, where able, the amount of any such commission in accordance with its disclosure obligations. The Client acknowledges receipt of the Adviser's Disclosure Statement.

6.Exclusivity

For the term of this mandate, the Client engages the Adviser exclusively in respect of arranging finance for the Transaction, and will not engage another broker or adviser, or approach lenders directly, in respect of the same finance without the Adviser's prior written agreement.

7.Term and termination

This mandate takes effect on signing and continues until the earlier of settlement of the Transaction or termination by either party on written notice. Termination does not affect the Client's obligation to pay the Adviser Fee where finance introduced or arranged by the Adviser proceeds to settlement, including within 6 months after termination.

8.Client obligations and warranties

The Client will provide the Adviser with true, accurate and complete information, will promptly provide any documents reasonably required, and will keep the Adviser informed of any material change in circumstances. Where the Client includes a company or trust, the person signing warrants that they are authorised to bind that entity to this mandate.

9.Privacy

The Adviser will handle the Client's personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 2020. Personal information is collected and held by Newton Financial Solutions Limited (trading as Agile Strategy) to arrange the finance and to meet the Adviser's legal obligations, and may be shared with the Lender and other parties as necessary for that purpose. The Client may request access to, and correction of, that information.

10.Liability

The Adviser is not liable for the Lender's decisions, the terms the Lender offers, or any delay or failure by the Lender or any other third party. Nothing in this mandate limits any rights the Client has that cannot be excluded by law.

11.Governing law

This mandate is governed by, and is to be interpreted in accordance with, New Zealand law.

12.Acknowledgement

The Client confirms that it has read and understood this mandate, and agrees to be bound by it.
