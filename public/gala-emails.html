4. What we say, and what we don't

Keep it about us: our club, our river, our harbour, our people.

Say it like this

  • "Sixty years of members heading out past the Heads, and we want to celebrate it properly, at home in our own clubrooms."
  • "Money raised on the night goes to water safety in Whakatāne and the Eastern Bay, and to the club's Next 40 Years Fund."
  • "Asanka is cooking, the bar is ours, the band is local. Every dollar stays in town."
  • "Your business is part of the club already. This is your chance to stand up in front of the people who back you all year."
  • "Nothing to pay yet. Just a yes, a maybe or a not this year."
  • "Our sponsors are the reason a member can come for $80."

Steer clear of

  • Promising a tax receipt or donation receipt. We are still confirming that, so say "we will let you know".
  • Talking about any club contract or tender. The invitation has no bearing on either, now or later.
  • Using the Wairaka version of the logo. Public material uses the Moutohorā logo only, as on this page.
  • Sharing member names, numbers or email addresses outside the gala team.
  • Pressure. A not this year is a fine answer, and a friendly one keeps the door open for next time.
  • Headcounts we can't yet promise. Say "a full room at the clubrooms", not a number.
