Is the gala on? We know by Wednesday 14 October.

Seven days, one question to every member business and contractor: can we count you in? Plus one click from every member. The room holds 220: 8 tables of ten and 140 member and guest seats.

Go

Business interest of about $35,000 + GST and 100 members saying they are in.

Go, with changes

One of the two numbers is short. Look at the ticket price, a smaller room or a cheaper band.

Stop and rethink

Business interest below about $19,000 (fixed costs) and fewer than 60 members. Nothing has been spent.
