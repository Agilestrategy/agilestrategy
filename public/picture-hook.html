On the wall

  1. Measure up from the floor to the hook height and make a light pencil mark.
  2. Fix the hook so the bearing point of the hook (where the wire actually rests) is on the mark, not the nail.
  3. For two or more pictures, use the Wall designer tab. It keeps the whole group centred at eye level and spaces the frames evenly.
