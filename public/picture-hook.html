Hook height = eye level + (frame height ÷ 2) − hang drop. With the figures above: 1500 + 450 − 120 = 1330

On the wall

  1. Measure up from the floor to the hook height and make a light pencil mark.
  2. Fix the hook so the bearing point of the hook (where the wire actually rests) is on the mark, not the nail.
  3. For two or more pictures, use the Wall designer tab. It keeps the whole group centred at eye level and spaces the frames evenly.

Measure a frame with your phone

Photograph each picture with an A4 sheet or a bank card on the glass. The scanner finds the frame edges in the photo, works out the size from the reference, and keeps the image for the wall preview. Add the hang drop from your tape and it joins the picture list below, ready for the wall designer.

Scanned and listed pictures

These are the same pictures the wall designer uses. Edit sizes or order there.
1 · Single picture
  1. Measure the frame height, top edge to bottom edge.
  2. Hang the picture off the hook end of your tape by its wire, let it take the weight, read the drop to the top of the frame.
  3. Type both in. Mark the wall at the hook height shown. 1500 mm centre is the default; change it if you like.
2 · Scan pictures
  1. Choose your reference (A4 sheet or bank card), lay it flat on the glass, and shoot square on with the whole frame in the photo.
  2. The page finds the frame edges and usually the reference and drops the dots in place. Drag any dot that's off, or tap the two reference ends if it asks. Then tap Measure.
  3. Check the size, add the hang drop from your tape, name it, add to wall. The photo shows on the wall preview.
3 · Wall designer
  1. Enter the wall size, the group centre height and the gap. Pick a layout: one row, a grid, or Free arrangement to drag frames anywhere.
  2. Gold dots are hook marks. Recentre and Centre on wall keep the group where it should be.
  3. Read across and up for each hook from the Hook marks table. Print it or copy it.
4 · Save, room by room
  1. Name the wall (Lounge, Hall) and press Save wall. Saved walls stay on this phone; open any of them later.
  2. Copy for Notes puts the hook list on the clipboard. Paste into Apple Notes or Google Keep, one note per room.
  3. Download gives a text file with the marks and a backup of the design. New wall starts fresh without touching saved walls.

Good to know

  1. Everything you enter stays in your own browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  2. The scan is accurate to a couple of percent when the reference sits flat on the glass and the photo is square on. For a frame that must be exact, check one side with the tape.
  3. Hook height means the bearing point of the hook, where the wire actually rests, not the nail.
